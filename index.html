Introduction

What is Nethxeum?

Nethxeum (NTU) is a decentralized, privacy-preserving cryptocurrency that combines the proven scarcity model of Bitcoin with the battle-tested cryptographic privacy of Monero. Founded by Rony Katie, it introduces a hard supply cap of 42,000,000 NTU, a Bitcoin-style halving schedule every 420,000 blocks, zero tail emission, and full transaction confidentiality enforced at the protocol level.

Privacy by Default

Every transaction on the Nethxeum network is private by default. Sender identity, recipient identity, and transaction amounts are all cryptographically concealed through Ring Signatures, RingCT, and Stealth Addresses. Privacy is not optional — it is mandatory.

Sound Money

With a fixed supply of 42 million NTU and no tail emission, Nethxeum implements true digital scarcity. The halving every 420,000 blocks creates a predictable, transparent monetary policy that anyone can verify and trust. No central authority can inflate the supply.

Tokenomics

Halving Schedule

Nethxeum follows a Bitcoin-inspired halving schedule. Every 420,000 blocks (~4 years), the block reward is cut in half. The total supply converges precisely to 42,000,000 NTU.

Epoch 1 — Blocks 0–419,999 (50 NTU/block) 21,000,000 NTU (50%)
Epoch 2 — Blocks 420,000–839,999 (25 NTU/block) 10,500,000 NTU (75% cumulative)
Epoch 3 — Blocks 840,000–1,259,999 (12.5 NTU/block) 5,250,000 NTU (87.5% cumulative)
Epoch 4 — Blocks 1,260,000–1,679,999 (6.25 NTU/block) 2,625,000 NTU (93.75% cumulative)
Epoch 5+ — Continuing halvings until 0 ~2,625,000+ NTU (100% at ~160 years)

Privacy Stack

Three-Layer Privacy

Nethxeum inherits Monero's cryptographic privacy stack — the most proven privacy technology in production cryptocurrency, tested for over a decade under adversarial conditions.

Ring Signatures

Your transaction is co-signed alongside decoy outputs from the blockchain. An observer cannot determine which member of the ring is the actual sender. Minimum ring size enforced at protocol level.

RingCT

Ring Confidential Transactions hide the value of every input and output using Pedersen Commitments. Bulletproofs provide compact range proofs. No transaction amount is visible to any third party.

Stealth Addresses

Each transaction generates a unique one-time address. Your published address never appears on the blockchain. Multiple payments cannot be linked to the same recipient.
