Avoid Scams

Protect yourself from scams and fraudulent schemes.

Warning: Scams Are Common in Cryptocurrency

Always verify information through official channels. When in doubt, ask the community.

Common Scams

  • Fake wallets: Only download from nethxeum.io or official GitHub. Verify checksums.
  • Phishing: Double-check URLs. Scammers use domains like "nethxeum-wallet.com" or "nethxeum-support.org".
  • Seed theft: NO ONE from the Nethxeum team will ever ask for your seed or private keys.
  • Investment schemes: Nethxeum is not an investment. Anyone promising guaranteed returns is a scammer.
  • Fake exchanges: Only use established exchanges. Verify through official announcements.

How to Stay Safe

  • Bookmark official sites and only use those bookmarks
  • Verify all download checksums
  • Never share your seed or keys
  • Check URLs carefully for typos
  • Ask in official Discord/Telegram before acting
  • Report suspicious activity to moderators
