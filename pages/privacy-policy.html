Privacy Policy

How the Nethxeum website handles your data.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Nethxeum was founded on the principle that privacy is a fundamental human right. This privacy policy reflects that commitment.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The Nethxeum website is designed to be privacy-respecting by default.

What We Don't Collect

  • No IP addresses
  • No cookies (except essential language preference)
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No advertising or third-party embeds
  • No server logs with identifying information

Local Storage

The only data stored is your language preference (e.g., "en", "fr") in your browser's localStorage. This is purely functional and contains no personal information. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

All website content is served statically. No databases, no user accounts, no tracking.
